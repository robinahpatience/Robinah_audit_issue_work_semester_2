Our story

A family kitchen, grown up

JK Restaurant started as a small food stall run by two sisters, Jacinta and Keziah, cooking lunch for market traders in Mukono town. Ten years on, the stall has become a full restaurant, but the kitchen still runs on the same idea: cook the food we grew up eating, and cook it properly.

Every luwombo is still wrapped in banana leaf and steamed for hours, not minutes. Our tilapia comes from suppliers on Lake Victoria most mornings. Where we can, ingredients are bought from the Mukono market a short walk from our door.

What we care about

Good food shouldn't be rushed, and it shouldn't be intimidating either. We want first-time visitors and long-time regulars to feel equally at home at our tables.
